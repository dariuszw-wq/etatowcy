**In December 2026 shops in Poland may open on three Sundays: 6, 13 and 20 December. You may work in retail on no more than two of them. The limit covers employment contracts, umowa zlecenia (civil-law contract of mandate) and temporary agency work.** From 24 to 27 December the trade ban applies. Check your schedule before the season starts.

## Which days are working days
The trade ban does not apply on the three consecutive Sundays before Christmas Eve (Art. 7(1)(1) of the Act). December 2026 looks like this:

- **6, 13 and 20 December** — trading Sundays (niedziele handlowe),
- **24 December (Thursday)** — Christmas Eve, a day off work,
- **25 and 26 December (Friday and Saturday)** — Christmas,
- **27 December** — a Sunday with the trade ban. In November the labour minister confirmed that there will be no extra trading Sunday on this day.

In retail, a Sunday or public holiday lasts **24 hours, from midnight to midnight** (Art. 3(7)). So the ban starts at midnight between 23 and 24 December and ends at midnight between 27 and 28 December. It also covers night shifts in a shop, and storing goods or stocktaking in a retail outlet.

## The two-Sunday limit: who it covers
The Act says that on the December trading Sundays a pracownik (employee) or zatrudniony (engaged person) may work in retail on **no more than two Sundays** (Art. 7(1b)). Both words have a broad meaning in the Act:

- **pracownik** — a person with an employment contract (umowa o pracę), including one assigned by a temporary work agency,
- **zatrudniony** — a person with a civil-law contract, for example umowa zlecenia, including one assigned by an agency.

Your nationality does not matter. The provision does not allow a third Sunday with the worker's consent or for extra pay.

## A day off for a Sunday
If you have an employment contract, you are entitled to **another day off** for every Sunday worked (Art. 151¹¹ of the Labour Code). The employer gives it within the 6 calendar days before or after that Sunday:

- for 6 December: 30 November – 5 December or 7–12 December,
- for 13 December: 7–12 December or 14–19 December,
- for 20 December: 14–19 December or 21–26 December. Note: 24, 25 and 26 December are public holidays, so they are days off anyway.

If this is not possible, you are entitled to the day off by the end of the settlement period (okres rozliczeniowy, the time over which the company settles your hours). Only if that is also impossible do you receive a **100% pay supplement for every hour** worked on Sunday. So money is not an ordinary substitute for the day off.

Apart from this, an employee should have a free Sunday at least **once every 4 weeks** (Art. 151¹²). The exception is a system of work only on Fridays, Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays.

On a zlecenie the Labour Code does not give you a day off for Sunday or the 100% supplement. Check whether your contract does. The two-Sunday limit applies to you all the same.

## Where the trade ban does not apply
Art. 6 of the Act lists 32 exceptions to the ban. They include petrol stations, pharmacies, online shops, outlets whose main activity is food service, bakeries and confectioners that mainly sell their own type of goods, and outlets at stations, to the extent that they serve travellers. The ban also does not cover an owner who trades in person.

## What this means for you
**If you work in a shop:**
- Check that your schedule does not include all three Sundays: 6, 13 and 20 December.
- Write down when you will get the day off for each Sunday.
- Keep a photo of the schedule and a list of the hours you worked.
- Do not agree to unpaid "help" on a Sunday or public holiday. Assigning such work in retail outlets without pay is prohibited (Art. 4).
- If the company breaks the rules, you can file a complaint with Państwowa Inspekcja Pracy (PIP, the National Labour Inspectorate).

**If you are an employer:**
- Divide the three Sundays so that nobody works more than two. Count zlecenie contractors and temporary workers too.
- Enter the days off for Sundays in the schedule together with the Sundays themselves.
- Do not plan retail work from 24 to 27 December unless your outlet falls under an exception in Art. 6.
- Assigning work in breach of the Sunday or holiday trade ban carries a fine of **PLN 1,000 to PLN 100,000** (Art. 10(1)).
