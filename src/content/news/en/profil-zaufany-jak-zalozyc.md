A Trusted Profile (profil zaufany) lets you confirm your identity in Polish public online services and sign documents with a trusted signature. It is useful for employees and jobseekers: you can use it to access services such as ZUS and the e-Tax Office. Registration and use are free. Foreign nationals can also have a profile: they need a PESEL number, must be at least 13 and have full or limited legal capacity.

There are two practical routes: through a bank that supports the service, or an online application followed by an in-person visit to a confirmation point.

## 1. Prepare your PESEL, identity document and contact details

Prepare your PESEL number, a valid identity document, your own email address and a phone you can access. At a confirmation point you can present, for example, a foreign passport or residence card. Your application details must match the document and the PESEL register.

If you do not have a number yet, start with the [official PESEL service for foreign nationals](https://www.gov.pl/web/gov/uzyskaj-numer-pesel-dla-cudzoziemcow). Creating an account on the PZ portal does not by itself mean your profile has been confirmed.

## 2. Register through your bank

1. Open the [official Trusted Profile registration page](https://pz.gov.pl/ui/au/register?isPzMode=true).
2. Choose “Bank lub inny dostawca” (bank or another provider), then select your bank from the list.
3. Sign in on the bank’s website and follow the instructions to confirm your details.
4. Once finished, check your PZ account to make sure the profile is valid.

Not every bank offers this method. Having a bank account does not guarantee that you can register this way. If the service is unavailable to you, use a confirmation point.

## 3. Apply online and visit a confirmation point

1. In [PZ registration](https://pz.gov.pl/ui/au/register?isPzMode=true), choose “Punkt potwierdzający” (confirmation point).
2. Enter your own details and follow the system’s instructions to verify your contact information. Keep a record of your username.
3. Choose an office. It does not have to be where you live or have your registered address. If a booking is required, arrange an appointment using the office’s specified method.
4. Attend in person with a valid identity document. The official will compare the details, prepare a printout of the application for you to sign and confirm the profile after successful verification.

**You have 30 days from submitting the application.** If you do not confirm your profile within this time, the application will be removed from the system (§ 3 ust. 2 rozporządzenia w sprawie profilu zaufanego i podpisu zaufanego). Someone who helps you fill in the form cannot attend identity confirmation on your behalf.

## 4. Find a confirmation point

Our [Trusted Profile confirmation directory](/en/trusted-profile-confirmation-points/) contains all **2,293 offices in Poland** copied from the COSC material. We have retained the original data date: **5 November 2026**. Overseas points are not included.

Search by town, name or address and filter by province. You can type without Polish accents, for example “Lodz”. Each entry includes its address and any organisational notes, map links and booking links provided in the source. The map searches for the listed address. Booking links open external services listed in the PZ directory. This is a dated snapshot, not a live register.

## 5. Check validity and protect your account

A Trusted Profile is valid for **3 years** and can be extended for another period of the same length. Keep access to your phone and email. Do not share your password, SMS codes or signature authorisation with an employer or intermediary. Help understanding a form does not require handing over control of your account.

A video call with an official is not available to everyone holding a foreign identity document. The official FAQ requires a valid Polish identity card or valid Polish passport; passport holders must previously have held a Polish identity card issued after 31 December 2000. A foreign passport or residence card is not a Polish e-ID either: having a chip alone does not make it suitable for this method.

If SMS codes do not arrive, check the phone number stored in your profile and your phone settings. Current instructions are in the [Trusted Profile FAQ](https://www.gov.pl/web/profilzaufany/najczesciej-zadawane-pytania2). PZ support: **+48 42 253 54 50**, working days **7:00–18:00 Polish time**.

## What this means for you

If your bank supports verification of your details, you can follow its online procedure. Otherwise, submit an application and schedule a visit within 30 days. Etatowcy is not a Trusted Profile confirmation point. You confirm your identity through an authorised provider or in person at an office.
