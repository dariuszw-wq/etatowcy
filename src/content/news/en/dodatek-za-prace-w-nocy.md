**Do you work night shifts? For every hour worked during the night period your employer must pay you a supplement (dodatek za pracę w porze nocnej) — no matter how much you earn.** The supplement is calculated from the minimum wage, so in November and December 2026 it is about PLN 6.01 gross per hour, and from January 2027 it will go up. Here we explain when work counts as "night work", how to calculate the supplement and who must not be employed at night.

## What the night period is
The night period (pora nocna) is **8 hours between 21:00 and 7:00** (Art. 151⁷ § 1 of the Labour Code). Which hours exactly — e.g. 22:00–6:00 or 23:00–7:00 — is decided by the employer in the work rules (regulamin pracy) (Art. 104¹ § 1 point 4). If the company has no work rules, the employer must state this in writing in the information on terms of employment (informacja o warunkach zatrudnienia).

This matters, because the supplement is due only for hours within that range. Example: the night period in your company is 22:00–6:00 and your shift runs from 18:00 to 2:00. You get the supplement for 4 hours (22:00–2:00), not for the whole shift.

## How much the night supplement is
For every hour of work during the night period you are owed a supplement of **20% of the hourly rate derived from the minimum wage** (Art. 151⁸ § 1). It is not calculated from your own salary but from the minimum wage — a person earning PLN 5,000 and a person earning PLN 9,000 get the same amount.

How to calculate the rate:
1. Take the minimum wage: **PLN 4,806 gross** in 2026, **PLN 4,950 gross** in 2027.
2. Divide it by the number of working hours in the given month (working time norm, wymiar czasu pracy). The norm is calculated under Art. 130: 40 hours for each full week plus 8 hours for each remaining day from Monday to Friday, minus 8 hours for each public holiday that does not fall on a Sunday.
3. Take 20% of the result.

Because the number of working hours changes from month to month, so does the supplement:

| Month | Working time norm | Supplement per 1 night hour |
|---|---|---|
| October 2026 | 176 h | approx. PLN 5.46 gross |
| November 2026 | 160 h | approx. PLN 6.01 gross |
| December 2026 | 160 h | approx. PLN 6.01 gross |
| January 2027 | 152 h | approx. PLN 6.51 gross |
| February 2027 | 160 h | approx. PLN 6.19 gross |

Example: in February 2027 you work 20 night shifts of 8 hours, entirely within the night period. The supplement will be 160 h × PLN 6.19 ≈ **PLN 990 gross** — on top of your normal pay.

The night supplement does not count towards the minimum wage. So the employer cannot "hide" it in a salary of PLN 4,806 and claim to be paying the minimum.

Exception: if you permanently work at night outside the workplace (e.g. as a driver), the supplement may be replaced by a monthly lump sum (ryczałt). The lump sum must correspond to the expected number of night hours (Art. 151⁸ § 2).

## "Night worker" — extra protection
If you have at least 3 hours of work in the night period every day, or at least 1/4 of your working time in the settlement period falls at night, you are a **night worker** (pracujący w nocy) within the meaning of the law (Art. 151⁷ § 2). Then:
- for particularly dangerous work or work involving heavy physical or mental effort you may work **no more than 8 hours a day** — a 12-hour night shift in such work is not allowed (Art. 151⁷ § 3). The list of such work is set by the employer,
- at your request the employer must inform the district labour inspector that it employs night workers (Art. 151⁷ § 6).

## Who must not be employed at night
- **Pregnant women** — an absolute ban (Art. 178 § 1). The employer must change her schedule to day work, move her to other work, and if that is impossible — release her from the duty to work while she keeps her previous pay (Art. 178¹ in conjunction with Art. 179 § 5).
- **Parents of children under 8** — only with their consent (Art. 178 § 2).
- **Juvenile workers (15–18 years old)** — banned; for them the night period is 22:00–6:00 (Art. 203).

## What this means for you

**If you are an employee:**
- Check in the work rules or in the information on terms of employment which hours count as the night period in your workplace.
- On your payslip (pasek wypłaty) look for the item "dodatek nocny" (night supplement) and compare the number of hours with your schedule.
- You are owed the supplement also when night is your normal shift, not overtime. If a night hour is also overtime, you get both supplements.
- The rules are the same for every employee on an employment contract (umowa o pracę), including foreigners. If you work through a temporary work agency, the agency pays the supplement.
- On a contract of mandate (umowa zlecenia) the Labour Code does not provide for a night supplement — the minimum hourly rate applies (PLN 31.40 gross in 2026, PLN 32.30 gross in 2027), unless your contract gives you more.

**If you are an employer:**
- Put the night period into the work rules or into the information on terms of employment.
- Recalculate the supplement every month according to the current working time norm, and from 1 January 2027 according to the minimum wage of PLN 4,950 gross.
- Draw up the list of particularly dangerous work and keep to the 8-hour limit for night workers in such work.
- When an employee informs you that she is pregnant, take her off night shifts immediately. Obtain consent to night work from parents of children under 8 before scheduling them for night shifts.
