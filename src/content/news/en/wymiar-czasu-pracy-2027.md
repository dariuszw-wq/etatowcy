**In 2027 a full-time employee working Monday to Friday has 2008 hours to work, i.e. 251 days of 8 hours.** The shortest month will be May (144 hours); the longest will be March, April and the summer months (176 hours each). Two public holidays fall on a Saturday, so for each of them your employer must give you another day off. Below you will find the working time (wymiar czasu pracy) for every month and an explanation of how to calculate it.

## How working time is calculated
Working time (wymiar czasu pracy) is the number of hours you have to work in a given settlement period (okres rozliczeniowy). The Labour Code gives a simple formula (Art. 130 § 1):
1. Multiply 40 hours by the number of full weeks in the period.
2. Add 8 hours for each remaining day from Monday to Friday.
3. Subtract 8 hours for each public holiday that falls on a day other than Sunday (Art. 130 § 2).

In practice: count the days from Monday to Friday, subtract the holidays falling from Monday to Saturday and multiply the result by 8 hours. The working time standard is 8 hours a day and on average 40 hours in an average five-day working week (Art. 129 § 1). For part-time work the hours are set proportionally — for example, on a half-time contract (1/2 etatu), half of the hours in the table.

## Public holidays in 2027
The Act on public holidays lists 14 holidays. In 2027 they fall as follows:
- **1 January (New Year's Day)** — Friday,
- **6 January (Epiphany)** — Wednesday,
- **28 March (Easter Sunday)** — Sunday; **29 March (Easter Monday)** — Monday,
- **1 May (Labour Day)** — Saturday,
- **3 May (Constitution Day)** — Monday,
- **16 May (Pentecost)** — Sunday,
- **27 May (Corpus Christi)** — Thursday,
- **15 August (Assumption of Mary)** — Sunday,
- **1 November (All Saints' Day)** — Monday,
- **11 November (National Independence Day)** — Thursday,
- **24 December (Christmas Eve)** — Friday,
- **25 December (Christmas Day)** — Saturday; **26 December (second day of Christmas)** — Sunday.

Holidays on a Sunday (Easter Sunday, Pentecost, 15 August and 26 December) do not reduce working time and do not give an extra day off.

## Working time in each month of 2027
Full time, work from Monday to Friday:
- **January** — 152 hours (19 days),
- **February** — 160 hours (20 days),
- **March** — 176 hours (22 days),
- **April** — 176 hours (22 days),
- **May** — 144 hours (18 days),
- **June** — 176 hours (22 days),
- **July** — 176 hours (22 days),
- **August** — 176 hours (22 days),
- **September** — 176 hours (22 days),
- **October** — 168 hours (21 days),
- **November** — 160 hours (20 days),
- **December** — 168 hours (21 days).

Total: **2008 hours, 251 working days**. With a quarterly settlement period the working time is: Q1 — 488 hours, Q2 — 496 hours, Q3 — 528 hours, Q4 — 496 hours.

## Two holidays on a Saturday: 1 May and 25 December
Since every holiday other than on a Sunday reduces working time by 8 hours, a Saturday holiday reduces it too — even though Saturday is already your day off. That is why your employer must give you **another day off** in the same settlement period. In 2027 this applies to two days:
- **1 May (Saturday)** — with a one-month settlement period the day off must fall in May 2027. Employers often choose Friday 28 May (after Corpus Christi) or another day, but it is their decision.
- **25 December (Saturday)** — with a one-month settlement period the day off must fall in December 2027, e.g. on 27 or 31 December.

The table above already includes these days — that is why May has only 18 working days and December 21.

## What this means for you

**If you are an employee:**
- Check your schedule (grafik) to see which day you get off for 1 May and 25 December 2027. If it is not there, ask your supervisor.
- Your monthly pay does not depend on the number of hours in the month — in May (144 hours) you get the same as in March (176 hours) if you have a fixed monthly salary.
- If you work more hours than the working time in the settlement period, the extra hours may be overtime (praca w godzinach nadliczbowych, Art. 151) — with a pay supplement or time off instead.
- Are you a temporary worker? The schedule is usually set by the company where you work, but direct your questions to the coordinator of the temporary work agency.
- On a civil-law contract of mandate (umowa zlecenia) the rules on working time do not apply — the number of hours comes from the contract, and for every hour you are owed at least the minimum hourly rate.

**If you are an employer:**
- Plan the 2027 schedules so that working time is not exceeded in any settlement period.
- Set the days off for 1 May and 25 December 2027 within the settlement periods in which these holidays fall, and enter them in the schedule or announce them to employees.
- In shift and weekend work, remember that a Saturday holiday reduces working time by 8 hours also for people who work on Saturdays.
