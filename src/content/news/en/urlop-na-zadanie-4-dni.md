**On-demand leave (urlop na żądanie) is not extra time off. It is 4 days of your normal annual leave that you can take on a date you choose, and your employer cannot refuse.** Before the end of the year it is worth checking how many of these days you still have.

## How on-demand leave works
Your employer must grant you, at your request and on the date you indicate, **no more than 4 days of leave in each calendar year** (Art. 167²). You do not have to give a reason or wait for the leave schedule.

These 4 days are **part of your annual leave entitlement** (20 or 26 days), not an addition to it. Once you use them, you simply have less regular leave left.

## When to ask
You make the request **no later than on the day the leave starts** (Art. 167²). The Labour Code (Kodeks pracy) does not set a form, but for your own protection keep a record: a message, an e-mail or an entry in the system, with date and time.

Practical tip: ask before your shift begins. If you know you will not come to work that day, say so as early as possible.

## The 4-day limit applies to you, not to the company
The total on-demand leave **cannot exceed 4 days in a calendar year, regardless of how many employers** you work for in consecutive employment relationships that year (Art. 167³). If you changed jobs in March and used 3 days at the old company, you have 1 day left at the new one.

## Pay and unused leave
- For leave days you receive **the pay you would have received had you worked** (Art. 172). Variable components (e.g. bonuses or allowances) may be calculated from the average of the 3 months before the month of leave. If earnings fluctuate significantly, that period may be extended to 12 months.
- Leave not used in a given year must as a rule be granted by 30 September of the following year, but **this rule does not apply to on-demand days** (Art. 168).

## What this means for you
**If you are an employee:**
- Count how many on-demand days you have used this year (with all employers).
- Make your request no later than on the day the leave starts and keep proof of it.
- If your employer refuses even though you asked in time and within the limit, you can ask the National Labour Inspectorate (Państwowa Inspekcja Pracy, PIP) for help.

**If you are an employer:**
- Do not make approval depend on a reason or on shift staffing. The provision sets no such condition.
- When hiring, ask how many on-demand days the employee has used with the previous employer this year (Art. 167³).
- Record on-demand days separately to monitor the limit.
