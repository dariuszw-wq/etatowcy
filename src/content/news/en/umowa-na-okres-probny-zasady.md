**A probationary contract (umowa na okres próbny) is the first employment contract, meant to let you and the employer check whether the cooperation makes sense. It is a full employment contract: you are entitled to at least the minimum wage, paid leave and ZUS contributions.** However, the law limits how long it can last and how many times it can be signed. Here are the key rules.

## How long the probation period can last
A probationary contract is concluded for **no longer than 3 months** (Art. 25 § 2 of the Labour Code). Shorter limits apply when, after probation, the employer plans a fixed-term contract (Art. 25 § 2²):
- **up to 1 month** — if the next contract is to last less than 6 months,
- **up to 2 months** — if the next contract is to last from 6 to less than 12 months,
- **up to 3 months** — in other cases, e.g. when the next contract is to be open-ended or for at least 12 months.

The parties may **extend the 1- and 2-month limits once, by no more than 1 month**, if justified by the type of work (Art. 25 § 2³). Such a clause must be included in the contract (Art. 29 § 1(6)(b)).

## What the contract must contain
A probationary contract, like any employment contract, is concluded **in writing** (Art. 29 § 2). Besides the type of work, place, pay and working time, it must state (Art. 29 § 1(6)):
- the length of the probation period or its end date,
- for a shorter probation period — how long the next fixed-term contract the parties intend to sign will last.

The parties may also agree that the probation period is **extended by the time of leave** or other justified absence, e.g. sick leave (Art. 25 § 2¹). Without such a clause in the contract, the probation period is not extended automatically.

## Can you sign a second probation period?
A new probationary contract with the same employee is allowed only if you are to do a **different type of work** (Art. 25 § 3). A second probation period in the same position is not compliant with the law.

## How a probationary contract ends
The contract ends by itself **when the probation period expires**. Before that, either party may terminate it with notice (Art. 30 § 2). The notice period is (Art. 34):
- **3 working days** — if the probation period does not exceed 2 weeks,
- **1 week** — if the probation period is longer than 2 weeks,
- **2 weeks** — if the probation period is 3 months.

Notice must be **in writing** (Art. 30 § 3) and include information about the right to appeal to the labour court (Art. 30 § 5). The employer **does not have to** give a reason for terminating a probationary contract — the duty to state a reason applies to fixed-term and open-ended contracts (Art. 30 § 4).

If the employer terminates the contract in breach of the rules, you are entitled to **compensation** equal to your pay for the time until the contract was due to end (Art. 50 § 1). If the employer applies too short a notice period, the contract still lasts until the end of the correct period and you are paid for that time (Art. 49).

## Probation, pregnancy and leave
- A probationary contract **longer than 1 month** that would end after the third month of pregnancy **is extended until the day of childbirth** (Art. 177 § 3).
- During pregnancy and parental-related leave, the employer, as a rule, cannot terminate the contract (Art. 177 § 1).
- For each month worked you acquire leave: in your first job ever — 1/12 of the annual entitlement (Art. 153 § 1). For leave not used by the end of the contract you are, as a rule, entitled to a cash equivalent (Art. 171 § 1).

## What this means for you

**If you are an employee:**
- Before signing, check the end date of the probation period in the contract and whether it is extended by leave or sickness.
- Do not agree to work “on trial” without a contract — work from day one should have a written basis.
- If you receive notice, count the notice period under Art. 34 and check that it is in writing.

**If you are an employer:**
- Match the length of the probation period to the planned next contract (1, 2 or 3 months) and state your intention to conclude that contract.
- Do not sign a second probationary contract for the same position.
- If you want the probation period to be extended by absences, state this clearly in the contract.
